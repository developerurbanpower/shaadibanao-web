Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ShaadiBanao matrimonial app.

Is ShaadiBanao free to use? +
Yes! Creating a profile, searching verified matches, receiving daily recommendations, and viewing initial Kundli compatibility scores is 100% free. Optional Premium subscriptions unlock unlimited direct messaging and contact unlocks.
How are matrimonial profiles verified? +
Every user must verify their phone number via secure OTP. Additionally, our automated verification system and manual trust & safety team inspect uploaded photos and offer Government ID (Aadhaar / Driving License) badges.
Can I keep my photos private or blurred? +
Absolutely. In your Privacy Settings, you can choose between 'Visible to Everyone', 'Blurred until requested', or 'Visible only to mutual accepted interests'.
How does the 36 Guna Kundli Milan work? +
Our algorithm uses classical Vedic astrology principles (Ashtakoot Milan) taking the Moon signs (Rashi) and birth stars (Nakshatra) of both boy and girl to evaluate compatibility across health, temperament, progeny, and marital longevity.
How can I delete my account or personal data? +
You can delete your account anytime directly within the mobile app under Settings -> Privacy -> Delete Account, or submit a request on our Account Deletion Request Page. All data will be scrubbed within 48 hours.
