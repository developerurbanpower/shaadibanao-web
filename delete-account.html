Account & Data Deletion Portal

Official web portal for users of ShaadiBanao (Package Name: com.shaadibanao.app) to request permanent account removal.

Google Play Developer Policy & DPDP Act Compliance: In accordance with Google Play’s user data policy and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the absolute right to delete your ShaadiBanao account and all associated personal data at any time, directly through this web page or within the mobile application.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you still have the ShaadiBanao app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the ShaadiBanao application.
  2. Tap on your Profile Avatar in the top navigation bar.
  3. Go to Settings ➔ Account & Security.
  4. Scroll down to Account Actions and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your mobile number with a quick OTP. Your profile and images will be permanently erased immediately.

Method 2: Web-Based Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, please fill out the official deletion request form below. Our Data Safety Team will process your request and permanently erase your account data within 48 hours.

Submit Account Deletion Request

Please enter the details associated with the registered ShaadiBanao account you wish to purge.

3. What Happens to Your Data Upon Deletion?

When an account is deleted through either Method 1 or Method 2, our automated data purge protocol executes the following actions:

  • Profile Data Eradication: Your name, bio, birth details, astrological charts, religion, caste, occupation, and family preferences are permanently scrubbed from our active databases.
  • Media Storage Purge: All profile photographs and government ID verification snapshots stored on our secure cloud buckets (AWS S3) are permanently destroyed.
  • Chat History & Interactions: All direct messages, expressions of interest, shortlists, and blocklists are permanently removed.
  • Search Index Invalidation: Your profile is immediately de-indexed and will never appear in search results, daily recommendations, or Kundli Milan comparisons.

4. Data Retention Exceptions (Legal & Regulatory Mandates)

In strict adherence to applicable Indian laws and regulatory guidelines, we retain limited transactional data as follows:

  • Financial & Invoicing Records: If you purchased a premium subscription, payment invoice records (GST invoice number, date, amount, payment transaction ID) are retained for 7 years solely to comply with the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Act and Income Tax Act, 1961. No credit card or banking details are retained by us.
  • Fraud & Security Prevention: In cases where an account was banned or flagged for criminal impersonation, financial extortion, or severe harassment, an encrypted hash of the identifier may be retained in our safety blacklist to prevent the bad actor from registering new accounts.

5. Need Direct Assistance?

If you experience any issues submitting your deletion request, you can email our Privacy Support Desk directly at privacy@shaadibanao.app or our Grievance Officer at grievance@shaadibanao.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - [Your Mobile Number]".
