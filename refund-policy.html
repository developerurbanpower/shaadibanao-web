Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Transparency Pledge: We strive to provide complete satisfaction to all members on ShaadiBanao. This policy outlines our subscription cancellation rules, refund eligibility, and payment terms.

1. Nature of Digital Matrimonial Services

ShaadiBanao provides intangible, digital matchmaking services. Upon upgrading to any Premium Subscription (e.g. Silver, Gold, Platinum VIP), benefits such as direct contact unlocks, enhanced visibility algorithms, and unlimited chat are activated immediately upon successful transaction completion.

2. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your recurring membership subscription at any time. When you cancel, you will continue to have full access to your premium benefits until the conclusion of your current billing period. No further recurring charges will be deducted from your account.

3. Refund Eligibility & Conditions

Because premium digital services are provisioned instantly, refunds are governed by the following criteria:

  • Double Deduction / Technical Billing Errors: If an amount has been debited more than once due to a payment gateway timeout or bank glitch, the duplicate transaction will be refunded 100% within 5–7 working days upon verification.
  • Service Downtime: If our platform experiences major server inaccessibility continuously exceeding 72 hours preventing you from accessing your paid account, prorated refund credit will be issued.
  • Change of Mind / Early Marriage: If you find a partner or choose not to use the service prior to your plan expiry, partial or full refunds cannot be processed once contact credits or chat features have been actively utilised.

4. In-App Purchases via Google Play / Apple App Store

If your subscription was purchased through Google Play In-App Billing or Apple App Store In-App Purchases, billing, cancellation, and refund requests are subject to Google Play’s or Apple’s respective refund policies and must be initiated through your Google Play / Apple ID subscriptions manager.

5. How to Request a Refund

For payments made directly via UPI, Net Banking, or Debit/Credit card gateways, please submit your refund inquiry within 7 days of payment by emailing our billing desk:

Email: billing@shaadibanao.app

Required Information: Registered Mobile Number, Order ID / Transaction Ref No., and Brief Reason for Request.

Turnaround Time: Our accounts team reviews and responds to all billing inquiries within 2 business days.
