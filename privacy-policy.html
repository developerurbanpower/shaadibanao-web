Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Important Notice: This Privacy Policy applies to the ShaadiBanao mobile application (Package Name: com.shaadibanao.app) and the website shaadibanao.app. We are deeply committed to safeguarding your personal and matrimonial data in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, Information Technology Act, 2000, and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Introduction & Overview

ShaadiBanao ("we", "us", or "our") operates the matrimonial platform designed to assist individuals and families in discovering potential matrimonial matches. We recognise that searching for a life partner requires sharing sensitive personal, astrological, and familial information. This Privacy Policy details what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is safeguarded, and the controls you have over your data.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to facilitate authentic matchmaking, verify account authenticity, and calculate astrological compatibility:

A. Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Authentication: Mobile phone number (verified via OTP) and optional email address.
  • Profile Identity: First name, last name, gender, date of birth, age, marital status, height, mother tongue, religion, caste, gotra, and bio.
  • Professional & Education Details: Highest education, college/university, employed in, occupation, and annual income band.
  • Family Details: Family values (Traditional, Moderate, Liberal), family type (Nuclear, Joint), and parents' occupations.
  • Astrological Details: Rashi (Moon sign), Nakshatra (birth star), Manglik status, date, time, and place of birth for 36 Gunas Kundli Milan calculation.
  • Partner Preferences: Desired age range, height, religion, caste, education, occupation, and city preferences.

B. Photographs & Camera Access

When you upload profile pictures, we request access to your device's camera or photo library. Photos are securely stored on certified cloud storage (Cloudinary) and are screened for decency. You retain complete privacy control over your photos (Public, Blurred until requested, or Visible only after mutual interest acceptance).

C. Device & Technical Information

  • IP address, device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for security logging and anti-fraud detection.
  • FCM Push Notification tokens to deliver instant match alerts and chat messages.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilised for lawful matrimonial matching purposes:

  1. To create and manage your matrimonial profile and authenticate your login.
  2. To evaluate compatibility algorithms and compute 36 Gunas Vedic Kundli Milan.
  3. To facilitate secure messaging and mutual interest connections between verified profiles.
  4. To protect our community against impersonation, spam, financial fraud, and harassment.
  5. To coordinate booking requests with certified Vedic Pandits for Muhurat ceremonies.

4. Contact Privacy & The BandhanSetu Shield

Your phone number and email address are never displayed publicly. By default:

  • Contact numbers appear masked (e.g. +91 98•••• ••210) to prevent unsolicited calls or harassment.
  • Direct contact details are only unlocked when both parties mutually express interest and explicitly agree to exchange contact information.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to marketing agencies or third-party advertisers. Data is shared strictly with essential service infrastructure:

  • SMS Gateway Providers: To deliver one-time OTP verification codes to your mobile number.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Database: Secure, encrypted cloud servers (MongoDB Atlas, AWS, Render) adhering to SOC-2 and ISO 27001 data protection standards.
  • Law Enforcement: We may disclose data if legally mandated by a valid court order or statutory Indian law enforcement directive.

6. Data Security & Storage

All communications between your mobile device and our backend servers are secured via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption with 256-bit SSL certificates. Passwords, auth tokens, and session credentials are encrypted at rest using industry-standard hashing algorithms (bcrypt/Argon2).

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion (Play Store Compliance)

You have full legal control over your personal data at all times:

  • Right to Rectification: You can edit and update any profile details, photos, or partner preferences anytime inside the app.
  • Right to Hide Profile: You can toggle 'Hide Profile' in Privacy Settings to take your profile offline temporarily without deleting it.
  • Right to Complete Erasure (Deletion): You can permanently delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the app (Settings ➔ Privacy ➔ Delete Account), or submit a request on our dedicated web portal: Account Deletion Request Page. Upon confirmation, all your profile data, chats, kundli charts, and uploaded photos are permanently purged from our servers within 48 hours.

8. Children's Privacy (Age Restriction)

ShaadiBanao is strictly a matrimonial matchmaking service for adults seeking marriage. In accordance with the Prohibition of Child Marriage Act and Indian legal marriage ages, our service is exclusively restricted to women aged 18 and older, and men aged 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit data from minors.
